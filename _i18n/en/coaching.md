landing-tekst: >-
  Do you want more self-insight, a better handle on your approach to certain matters, to break free from certain patterns, or concrete tools to be more effective in your work?


  With individual coaching, we help you in a tailored way. In a manner (live, outdoors, online, on the job) and rhythm (a quick boost or rather a calmer, somewhat longer trajectory?) that suits you.


  Based on an intake, you choose the coach that suits you, and together you determine what the coaching trajectory looks like. We tailor every coaching trajectory to your needs, goals, and context. On one hand, this involves depth: what is the background of your coaching question? And on the other hand, practical application: translating the insights you gain into your life and work.


  We coach from the work floor up to the executive level. Independently and confidentially. It can be linked to an organizational development trajectory or leadership development. But also for individuals looking for more balance or the next career step.

coaching-tekst: >-
  **Coaching Themes**


  The goal and theme of the coaching trajectory are always individually determined and often a combination of multiple elements. In practice, we often work with the following themes:


  * Personal development * Communication * Balance and resilience | stress, burnout * Career development


  \

  **Methods and Forms** \

  We work with methods and materials from various management theories, psychology, and approaches such as Voice Dialogue, Co-active coaching, ACT, Insights, and Deep Democracy. \

  \

  In a coaching trajectory, various conversation forms and elements can be used, such as: conversations (live, online, or walking), in-depth assignments, practical assignments, coaching on the job, three-way conversations with the manager, reading material, background articles, reflection assignments, tests, and videos.


  **Always Custom-Made** \

  We help you gain insight into your behavioral patterns, explore your comfort zone, and implement sustainable changes. This way, you work not only on your professional growth but also on a healthier balance and more resilience.


  So, we don't do 'standard programs'. It's not a matter of 'give me module 2 and session type x'. Instead, we look together with you at what works. In your context, with your goals, wishes, and own wisdom. \

  \

  **The Coaches** \
